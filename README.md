## About the Project

**BrandGuard AI** is an AI-powered advertising compliance platform designed to help businesses, marketers and creative teams identify potential mistakes and compliance issues in advertising materials before publication.

The platform analyses uploaded marketing creatives and their content to identify issues such as spelling mistakes, incorrect pricing, misleading claims, offer inconsistencies, branding errors and visual discrepancies.

It aims to make advertisement reviewing faster, easier and more reliable through AI-powered image understanding and text analysis.

## Key Features

- **AI-Powered Advertisement Analysis:** Analyse uploaded advertising creatives.
- **Image Understanding:** Examine visual elements in advertisements using AI vision capabilities.
- **OCR-Based Text Detection:** Extract and inspect text appearing inside images.
- **Error Detection:** Identify potential spelling, pricing, promotional and content-related issues.
- **Logo Detection and Verification:** Identify logos and compare them with available official brand references.
- **Visual Error Highlighting:** Display detected issues at their corresponding locations on the advertisement using bounding boxes and numbered markers.
- **Compliance Findings:** Present detected issues with explanations, severity levels and suggested corrections.
- **Analysis History:** Maintain access to saved analysis results where supported by the application.

## How It Works

1. **Upload:** Upload an advertisement or marketing creative.
2. **Image Analysis:** AI examines the visual content of the uploaded image.
3. **Text Extraction:** OCR extracts visible text from the creative.
4. **Compliance Review:** The extracted text and visual information are analysed for potential issues.
5. **Findings:** Detected issues are presented with explanations and suggested corrections.
6. **Visual Review:** Issues with reliable location coordinates can be highlighted directly on the uploaded image.

## Issues the Platform Aims to Detect

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Spelling and Grammar | Misspelled words and grammatical mistakes |
| Pricing | Incorrect or inconsistent prices |
| Promotional Offers | Discount and offer inconsistencies |

| Dates and Duration | Incorrect campaign dates or offer periods |
| Branding | Logo inconsistencies and visual branding issues |
| Visual Content | Potential mismatches between images and advertising text |

## Project Objective

The objective of BrandGuard AI is to reduce manual effort in advertising reviews, identify potential errors before publication and help businesses maintain consistency across their marketing communications.

## Disclaimer

BrandGuard AI is an AI-assisted review tool. Its findings should be treated as recommendations for human review, not as a guarantee of legal or regulatory compliance. Final approval should be performed by an appropriate reviewer.
